# Directive 01: Portfolio and Platform Profiles

## 1. Portfolio Structure (Web/Carrd/Notion)
Your portfolio must contain 5 sections:
- **Hero Statement:** Who you are + what you offer in 2 sentences.
- **Service Menu:** Clear packages with prices or 'starting from' rates (AI & Automation, Media Production, Social Media).
- **Work Samples:** Minimum 6 pieces (2 per niche).
  - AI: JSON prompt system, API config walkthrough, Notion/Loom workflow, AI Strategy Brief.
  - Media: Short-form videos, before/after photo edits, YouTube thumbnails.
  - Social: 30-day content calendar, analytics report, 5 sample posts.
- **Process & Tools:** Show tech stack (ChatGPT, Claude, Premiere Pro, Make.com, Zapier, Canva).
- **Testimonials / Social Proof:** LinkedIn recommendations or certificates.

## 2. Upwork Optimization (Primary Income Platform)
- **Title:** "AI Systems & Media Virtual Assistant | 24/7 Availability | $25/hr"
- **Overview:** Lead with the problem. Example: "Busy founders lose 20+ hours/week to content, admin, and tech setup. I eliminate that."
- **Badges/Skills:** Turn on '24/7 availability'. Tag AI tools, video editing, social media.

### The 150-Word Winning Proposal
1. **Name ONE specific problem** you noticed in their job post.
2. **Explain exactly how you solve it.**
3. **Attach 1 relevant sample** or case study.
4. **Ask ONE specific question** about their project.
5. **Short CTA:** "Can we jump on a 15-min call this week?"

## 3. Fiverr Optimization (Passive Lead Machine)
Create these 5 gigs:
1. AI chatbot setup with custom JSON prompts (Starting $75)
2. Short-form video editing for Instagram/TikTok (Starting $35)
3. Social media management for one week (Starting $120)
4. AI workflow configuration using APIs (Starting $150)
5. Virtual assistant for 10 hours (Starting $200)

## 4. LinkedIn Optimization (Inbound Engine)
- **Headline:** "Virtual Assistant | AI Configuration & Automation | Video & Photo Editing | Social Media Strategy | 24/7 Availability"
- **Banner:** Canva design showing 3 service pillars.
- **About section:** Tell a story — why you do this, who you help, results.
- **Featured section:** Pin your portfolio link, a sample, and a testimonial.
- **Experience:** Add "Freelance Virtual Assistant" as current role.
