# Directive 04: Social Media Strategy

## 1. Instagram Strategy
- **Setup:** Switch to Creator/Business account. Link in bio = Portfolio URL.
- **Bio:** "AI-Powered VA | Video + Photo + Social Media | 24/7 Available | Book below"
- **Content Pillars (Post 4-5x/week):**
  1. Skill showcase (Time-lapses of editing)
  2. Tips & Value ("3 things my AI setup does for my clients")
  3. Behind the scenes (Your workflow/tools)
  4. Social proof (Client wins/testimonials)
  5. Personality (Show who you are)

## 2. X (Twitter) Strategy
- **Daily:** 1 Value tweet (tip/insight) + 1 Engagement tweet (reply to trending).
- **Weekly:** 1 Thread (e.g., "How I built an AI prompt system for a client in 2 hours").
- **Search:** Search keywords daily like "need virtual assistant", "hire social media manager".

## 3. TikTok Strategy
- **Cadence:** Post 1 video/day.
- **Content:** Short tutorials, before/after edits, AI tool demos.
- **Hook Formula:** "3 things I do as an AI VA that no one else offers..."
