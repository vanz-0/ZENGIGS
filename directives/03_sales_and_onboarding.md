# Directive 03: Sales and Onboarding

## 1. The 15-Minute Discovery Call Framework
1. **Open:** "Tell me about your business and what you are currently spending most of your time on."
2. **Dig:** "What happens if that problem is not solved in the next 90 days?"
3. **Value:** "Based on what you have told me, here is exactly how I would help you..."
4. **Offer:** Present your most relevant package — lead with outcomes, not hours.
   - Starter Pack: $480/mo (20 hrs)
   - Growth Pack: $940/mo (40 hrs)
   - Premium 24/7: $1,800/mo (80 hrs)
   - Project-Based: From $150
5. **Close:** "Does this sound like it would solve the problem? Shall we get started this week?"

## 2. Handling Objections

**"Your rate is too high"**
*Response:* "I understand budget is a consideration. What I can offer is a 5-hour trial project at a discounted rate so you can see the value before committing. Most clients find they save more in time than they spend in fees."

**"I need someone with more experience"**
*Response:* "Here are 3 samples directly relevant to your project. I would also offer a 100% satisfaction guarantee on our first project — if you are not happy with the output, I will redo it at no charge."

**"I need to think about it"**
*Response:* "Completely fair! What is the one thing holding you back? I want to make sure I have answered every concern before you decide." (Listen and address directly).

## 3. Onboarding & First Project
- Ask for a testimonial and LinkedIn recommendation within 48 hours of completing the first project.
- If no clients by Day 10, lower the first Upwork gig to $18-$20/hr just to get your first 5-star review, then raise it back to $25/hr.
