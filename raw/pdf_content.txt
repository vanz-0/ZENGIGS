WHY UPWORK FIRST
Upwork has buyers actively searching and ready to pay. It is the fastest path to your first $25/hr booking.
Profile Checklist
Profile photo: Professional, well-lit, plain background, direct eye contact
Title: 'AI Systems & Media Virtual Assistant | 24/7 Availability | $25/hr'
Overview (first 2 lines CRITICAL): Lead with a problem you solve, not your CV. Example: 'Busy
founders lose 20+ hours/week to content, admin, and tech setup. I eliminate that.'
Specialised profiles: Create 3 specialised profiles — one per niche (Upwork allows this)
Skills: Tag AI tools, video editing software, social media platforms specifically
Portfolio: Upload all work samples directly — visual proof converts best
Certifications: Add free certs (Google, Meta, HubSpot) before launching
Availability badge: Turn on '24/7 availability' — your key differentiator
Winning Proposal Formula
THE 150-WORD PROPOSAL
Line 1: Name ONE specific problem you noticed in their job post.
Line 2: Briefly explain exactly how you solve it.
Line 3: Attach 1 relevant sample or case study.
Line 4: Ask ONE specific question about their project.
Line 5: Short CTA — 'Can we jump on a 15-min call this week?'
Target: under 150 words. Long proposals get skipped.

10. DISCOVERY CALL & OBJECTION
HANDLING
Your Discovery Call Framework (15–20 min)
1.Open: 'Tell me about your business and what you are currently spending most of your time on.'
2.Dig: 'What happens if that problem is not solved in the next 90 days?'
3.Value: 'Based on what you have told me, here is exactly how I would help you...'
4.Offer: Present your most relevant package — lead with outcomes, not hours
5.Close: 'Does this sound like it would solve the problem? Shall we get started this week?'
Handling Common Objections
"Your rate is too high"
Response: 'I understand budget is a consideration. What I can offer is a 5-hour trial project at a
discounted rate so you can see the value before committing. Most clients find they save more in time
than they spend in fees.'
"I need someone with more experience"
Response: 'Here are 3 samples directly relevant to your project. I would also offer a 100% satisfaction
guarantee on our first project — if you are not happy with the output, I will redo it at no charge.'
"I need to think about it"
Response: 'Completely fair! What is the one thing holding you back? I want to make sure I have
answered every concern before you decide.' Then listen and address it directly.
